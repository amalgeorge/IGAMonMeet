# Meetings never become read-only "Final"; an Edit window closes instead

Meetings have no approval workflow and no Final state. Any User may edit a Meeting until 23:59 SGT two days after its Meeting date; after that only an Admin may. Preparing and Held are information-only labels. A Version taken at 23:59 SGT on the Meeting date records what was presented, and the Audit log records every later change, so history is kept without locking.

## Considered Options

- Draft / In review / Final with sign-off per team: rejected as too heavy for a monthly operational meeting.
- Editable forever by anyone: rejected because past records could be rewritten without an Admin.
