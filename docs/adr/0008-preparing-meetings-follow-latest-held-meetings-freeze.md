# 8. Preparing Meetings follow the latest Template catalogue and Theme; Held Meetings freeze

A Meeting keeps the Template version it was created from (v1). In v2 an Admin can mark Sections and KPIs Available and change Themes at any time, often mid-month. We decided that while a Meeting is Preparing, editors may switch on Available items from the **latest** Template version and the Meeting follows the **latest** version of its Theme; from the Meeting date (Held) both are frozen at the versions in use. This lets a change made mid-month reach this month's Meeting, while decks that were presented keep looking and reading exactly as they did. The alternative, pinning everything to the creation-time version, was simpler but forced every mid-month change to wait a whole month.
